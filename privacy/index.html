Privacy Policy

1. Nature of This Website

This website is an online informational product catalogue for MSR Tent Mart. There is no shopping cart, no user account registration, no online checkout, and no payment processing functionality on this website. The site does not present contact forms, newsletter sign-ups, or input fields that prompt visitors to enter personal information.

2. WhatsApp Enquiries

When a visitor chooses to tap or click an enquiry button on a product card or contact link, the website opens WhatsApp with a prefilled text message that references the selected product name and product code.

The prefilled message is not sent automatically; it is transmitted only if the visitor actively decides to press Send within their WhatsApp application. Once an enquiry is sent in WhatsApp, the communication takes place directly between the user and MSR Tent Mart on WhatsApp. At that stage, WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to the chat, and MSR Tent Mart will receive the sender's phone number, display name, and message contents.

3. Direct Email Communications

If an official email address is published on the site and a visitor chooses to send an email to MSR Tent Mart, the business receives the information provided in that email (such as the sender's email address, name, and message content) for the purpose of answering the enquiry.

4. Third-Party Resources & Technical Data

To ensure reliable presentation, performance, and typography, this website loads certain external assets from third-party content delivery networks:

  • Google Fonts: Web typography loaded from Google servers (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com).
  • Bootstrap CDN: Responsive styling framework loaded from cdn.jsdelivr.net.
  • Font Awesome CDN: Icons loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com.
  • GitHub Pages: Web hosting platform provided by GitHub.

As part of standard Internet protocols, when your browser requests these resources or visits the website, the third-party providers and hosting infrastructure automatically receive technical connection data, including your IP address, browser type, and timestamp, to serve the requested files.
